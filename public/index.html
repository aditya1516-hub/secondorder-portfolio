THE CHALLENGE

The best-looking move can still be the wrong next move.

Expansion needs capacity. Discounts have a margin cost. A quick crisis response can create a second problem. The context matters as much as the option.

01

Diagnose before acting

Read the company position and choose the problem that matters first.

02

Think beyond the first effect

Balance short-term results with trust, capacity and future constraints.

03

Defend your reasoning

Connect a choice to evidence, assumptions and the trade-off you accept.

04

Adapt as the case changes

Use feedback, stakeholder views and emerging issues to reconsider your plan.

These are practice objectives, not claims of measured learning improvement.
