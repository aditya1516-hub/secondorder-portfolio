Your four working spaces

One business.
Four ways to understand it.

Start on Scorecards. Move to the Strategy Desk to act. Use the Strategy Room and boardroom when the situation needs a closer look.

01 / Scorecards

Read the company first.

Compare revenue momentum, margin health, board confidence and consumer trust. Use the trend, supporting indicators, newsroom and simulated stock movement to choose your priorities.

  • Look at the whole business, not one rising number.
  • Some indicators measure pressure: lower can be better.
  • Return after each move to understand the cumulative change.

02 / Strategy Desk

Turn a priority into a move.

Choose one or two actions across growth, finance, product, channels, operations and more. Read the trade-offs, implement a decision, then follow its consequence chain.

  • Check cash, service and supply readiness before committing.
  • Explain your thinking when the board asks why.
  • A good intention does not guarantee a good outcome.

03 / Strategy Room

Respond when the plan meets pressure.

An emergency can interrupt the session. Read the situation, choose a response, consider a public statement and make the strategic calls needed to contain it.

  • Check the facts before making a public commitment.
  • Balance response speed with stakeholder impact.
  • Your emergency choices and reasoning become review evidence.

Before you enter

A few practical
questions.

When does the timer start?

Only after the in-simulation case brief and guided tour, when you select Start Playing. Browsing this website does not start a session. The current pilot uses a seven-minute decision window; it is a compressed prototype, not a full-length programme.

Do I need an API key?

Not for standard mode. AI-assisted mode requires a usable key and sufficient credits with the configured provider. You choose the mode on the existing simulation entry screen.

Is this live company information?

No. The featured case is an independently developed educational scenario. Market movement and business metrics are simulated, not live investment data.

Is the assessment a certification?

No. It is feedback on recorded simulation behavior. Expert calibration and participant validation are still required before making stronger assessment claims.

Can I bring an existing save?

Export it from your previous simulation, then use Import in the hosted simulation. Keep your original file as a backup.
